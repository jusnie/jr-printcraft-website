Admin Dashboard

Finish setup first

This admin dashboard needs your database connected before it can work. Open supabase-config.js and fill in your SUPABASE_URL and SUPABASE_ANON_KEY, then run the SQL in supabase-admin-setup.sql once inside your project's SQL Editor. Full steps are in the comments at the top of supabase-config.js.
